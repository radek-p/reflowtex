\pagetitle[Typesetting]{Alignments}
\bigskip

Multi-line derivations stay aligned on their relation signs, matrices set as
\TeX{} builds them, and equation numbers keep their place. Every row of an
alignment is evaluated at one measure, so its columns stay in step as it
narrows.
