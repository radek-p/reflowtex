\pagetitle[Typesetting]{Alignments}
\bigskip

Multi-line derivations stay aligned on their relation signs, matrices look
as they do in print, and equation numbers stay in place. All rows of an
alignment are fitted to the same width, so the columns stay aligned as the
text narrows.
