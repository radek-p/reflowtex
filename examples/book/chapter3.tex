\chapter{Beyond}
Theorems~\ref{thm:induction} and~\ref{thm:squares} are in the two earlier
chapters, and their numbers are correct here because the book was
compiled as one document.
\begin{theorem}
There is no largest $n \in \N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $n$ were largest, $n + 1 \in \N$ would be larger.
\end{proof}
