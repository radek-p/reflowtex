\section*{Displayed equations}
Displayed equations keep \TeX{}'s spacing, operator sizing, and limit placement:
\[
  \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi},
  \qquad
  \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6},
  \qquad
  \zeta(s) = \prod_{p\ \mathrm{prime}} \frac{1}{1 - p^{-s}}.
\]
Nested subscripts, roots and large delimiters are sized as in print:
\[
  \lim_{n \to \infty} \left( 1 + \frac{x}{n} \right)^{\! n} = e^{x},
  \qquad
  \sqrt[3]{\,\frac{-q}{2} + \sqrt{\frac{q^2}{4} + \frac{p^3}{27}}\,}.
\]
