\section*{A TikZ picture}
A completed TikZ box is captured to a tightly sized PDF page, converted to
inline SVG, and placed back in the text with \TeX{}'s own metrics --- so it can
sit anywhere a box can, here as a displayed automaton inside an \texttt{align*}:
\begin{align*}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=Stealth, baseline=(q0.base), thick]
  \node[draw, circle] (q0) at (0,0) {$q_0$};
  \node[draw, circle] (q1) at (2.4,0) {$q_1$};
  \node[draw, circle, double] (q2) at (4.8,0) {$q_2$};
  \draw[->, blue] (q0) -- node[above] {$a$} (q1);
  \draw[->, blue] (q1) -- node[above] {$b$} (q2);
  \draw[->, red]  (q1) to[bend left=40] node[below] {$a$} (q0);
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{align*}

The same box-level capture supports front ends such as \texttt{tikz-cd} without
requiring PGF source externalisation:
\[
\begin{tikzcd}
  A^* \arrow[r, bend left, "f"] \arrow[r, bend right, "g"']
    & B^* \arrow[r, hookrightarrow, "\iota"] & \mathbb{R}
\end{tikzcd}
\]
