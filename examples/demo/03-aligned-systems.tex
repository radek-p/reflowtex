\section*{Aligned systems and matrices}
Multi-line derivations align on their relation symbols, and matrices set exactly
as \TeX{} builds them:
\begin{align*}
  (f \circ g)'(x) &= f'\bigl(g(x)\bigr)\,g'(x), \\
  \frac{d}{dx} \int_{a}^{x} f(t)\,dt &= f(x), \\
  \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}^{-1}
    &= \textcolor{blue}{\frac{1}{ad - bc}}\begin{pmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{pmatrix}.
\end{align*}
The following numbered alignment exercises several independently aligned
columns, unequal rows, and equation numbers:
\begin{align}
  a&=b & ccccccc^7&=d & e&=f \\
  g&=h & i&=j & k&=\text{long entry}
\end{align}
A piecewise definition, using a \texttt{cases} environment (and a little colour,
which the theme switcher remaps per palette):
\[
  \operatorname{sgn}(x) =
  \begin{cases}
    \textcolor{blue}{+1} & \text{if } x > 0, \\
    \phantom{+}0 & \text{if } x = 0, \\
    \textcolor{red}{-1} & \text{if } x < 0.
  \end{cases}
\]
