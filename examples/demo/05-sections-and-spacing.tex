\section*{Sections and vertical spacing}
This heading, and the space it opens beneath itself, are typeset by \TeX{}: a
\LaTeX{} sectioning command carries its own font, size, and the vertical skips
above and below it, and Reflow TeX keeps them.

\vspace{1.5cm}

The gap above this sentence is an explicit \verb|\vspace{1.5cm}|. It keeps its
size, while the paragraphs around it still reflow to the width of the column.

\subsection*{A subsection}
Subsection headings are smaller and have less space around them. Both come
from the document class.
