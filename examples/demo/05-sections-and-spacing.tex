\section*{Sections and vertical spacing}
This heading, and the space it opens beneath itself, are typeset by \TeX{}: a
\LaTeX{} sectioning command carries its own font, size, and the vertical skips
above and below it, and Reflow TeX preserves them.

\vspace{1.5cm}

The gap above this sentence is an explicit \verb|\vspace{1.5cm}|. Author-requested
vertical space is reproduced exactly, while the paragraphs on either side still
reflow to fit whatever column width the page provides.

\subsection*{A subsection}
Subsection headings are smaller and open less space --- again straight from the
document class, not an approximation of it.
