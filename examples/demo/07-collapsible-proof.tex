\section*{A collapsible proof}
The companion package \texttt{reflowtex.sty} adds a few environments for
documents written with the web in mind. \texttt{accordion} is one: a block
that shows one of its panes at a time, switched by links inside them. Here a
one-line idea stands in for the proof until the reader asks for more; a PDF
of the same source prints the proof.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:demo-sq}
For every integer $n$, the square $n^2$ leaves remainder $0$ or $1$ on division
by $4$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{accordion}
\begin{collapsedpane}
\noindent\textit{Proof idea.} Split into even and odd $n$.
\expandlink[See the proof]
\end{collapsedpane}
\begin{expandedpane}
\begin{proof}\hfill\collapselink\par
Every integer is even or odd, so $n = 2k$ or $n = 2k+1$ for some integer $k$,
and
\[
  (2k)^2 = 4k^2,
  \qquad
  (2k+1)^2 = 4\bigl(k^2+k\bigr) + 1 .
\]
The first leaves remainder $0$ and the second remainder $1$.
\end{proof}
\end{expandedpane}
\end{accordion}
Inside, both panes are typeset by \TeX{} like everything else, and re-break
with the column.
