\section*{Inline mathematics}
The Knuth--Plass algorithm chooses line breaks by minimising the total
demerits of a paragraph. Reflow TeX runs it in the browser, not at compile time,
so this paragraph fits whatever width the page gives it, while the inline
mathematics keeps \TeX's spacing: Euler's identity
$e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$, the Cauchy--Schwarz bound
$\abs{\langle x, y\rangle} \le \norm{x}\,\norm{y}$, the golden ratio
$\varphi = \tfrac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}$, a binomial coefficient $\binom{n}{k}$, and a
quantified statement $\forall\,\varepsilon > 0\ \exists\,\delta > 0$. Resize
the window and the lines re-break around the formulas.
