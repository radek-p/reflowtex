The segment modality $\segment_X\, \varphi$ cuts a word at the positions in
$X$ and asks $\varphi$ to hold on every piece. It nests like any
quantifier, so
\[
  \exists p\; \exists X\; \segment_X\; \exists a\;
  \bigl(p \not\sim a \land \forall p'\,(a \le p' \lor p' \sim p)\bigr)
\]
says that the word splits into pieces, each with a value $a$ that differs
from the one at $p$ and comes first among the values of its piece.
