% Boxes saved with \saveboxresource and drawn with \useboxresource: one with
% text, drawn twice, and one narrower than its text, which a resource cuts to
% its box as a PDF form does, so the end of its text is not drawn.
\documentclass{article}
\begin{document}
\setbox0=\hbox{\itshape saved once, drawn twice}
\saveboxresource0
\edef\twice{\the\lastsavedboxresourceindex}
\setbox0=\hbox{cut to forty points}
\wd0=40pt
\saveboxresource0
\edef\cut{\the\lastsavedboxresourceindex}
A paragraph with a box resource in it, \useboxresource\twice, and the same
resource again a few words later, \useboxresource\twice, then text enough
to break the lines again at another width.

A box resource narrower than its text, [\useboxresource\cut], between
brackets, in a paragraph long enough to fill a line and then a few more
words after it.
\end{document}
