\begin{document}

A paragraph after the clear page, long enough to break onto a second line of
the column, ending in a descender: gyp.

A paragraph after a clear double page, which made a blank page, ending gyp.

\section{A heading after a clear page}
A paragraph under the heading, ending in a descender: gyp.

\vspace*{20pt}
A paragraph after a new page and a starred vertical space, ending gyp.

A paragraph after an empty page made with null and a new page, ending gyp.

A paragraph after two clear pages in a row, ending with a display:
\[ a = b_q + c \]

A paragraph after a new page straight after the display, ending gyp.
