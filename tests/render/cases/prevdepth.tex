% Interline glue that is not the lines' own: a \prevdepth the document assigns, \nointerlineskip, and an empty \hbox{} or \null in vertical mode between paragraphs.
\documentclass{article}
% one page with nothing but the text: checked against plain lualatex too (cases.ts)
\pagestyle{empty}
\begin{document}
A first paragraph, long enough to fill a line of the text width and then a
few more words, with a descender to end on: quickly.
\par\prevdepth=0pt
A paragraph after \verb|\prevdepth=0pt|, as a class sets it after a box, long
enough to break onto a second line of the column.
\par\prevdepth=.3\baselineskip
A paragraph after \verb|\prevdepth=.3\baselineskip|, as a framed box sets it,
long enough to break onto a second line of the column, gladly.
\par\nointerlineskip
A paragraph after \verb|\nointerlineskip|, which sets no glue between the
lines above and this one, long enough to break onto a second line.
\par\hbox{}
A paragraph after an empty \verb|\hbox{}| in vertical mode, which TeX stacks as
a box of its own, long enough to break onto a second line, gladly.

\null
A paragraph after \verb|\null| alone in vertical mode, long enough to break
onto a second line of the column, which ends the page.
\end{document}
