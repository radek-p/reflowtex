% Paragraphs under microtype whose last line ends in a full stop followed by a
% box holding only a box holding a penalty, the anchor a restated theorem
% leaves (thm-restate with hyperref). TeX's search for the character that
% protrudes goes into the box, finds nothing but penalties, steps back out and
% goes on before it: the full stop hangs into the margin. Each line overfills
% the measure by 0 to 3.5 pt before protrusion, so the protrusion decides
% whether it has to shrink; the last paragraph, without the box, is the
% control.
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage[T1]{fontenc}
\usepackage{lmodern}
\usepackage{microtype}
\newcommand\T{The result stated again below follows from the two lemmas above.}
\newcommand\A{\penalty10000\hbox{\raise13pt\hbox{\penalty10000}}}
\setbox0\hbox{\T}
\begin{document}
\noindent\hspace*{\dimexpr\linewidth-\wd0+0.0pt\relax}\T\A

\noindent\hspace*{\dimexpr\linewidth-\wd0+0.5pt\relax}\T\A

\noindent\hspace*{\dimexpr\linewidth-\wd0+1.0pt\relax}\T\A

\noindent\hspace*{\dimexpr\linewidth-\wd0+1.5pt\relax}\T\A

\noindent\hspace*{\dimexpr\linewidth-\wd0+2.0pt\relax}\T\A

\noindent\hspace*{\dimexpr\linewidth-\wd0+2.5pt\relax}\T\A

\noindent\hspace*{\dimexpr\linewidth-\wd0+3.0pt\relax}\T\A

\noindent\hspace*{\dimexpr\linewidth-\wd0+3.5pt\relax}\T\A

\noindent\hspace*{\dimexpr\linewidth-\wd0+1.5pt\relax}\T

Running text whose lines end in boxes of nothing: a line may end in such a box\A{} after a full stop.\A{} Another
sentence follows it, and then another,\A{} with a comma before the box, and so on until the paragraph has a few
lines.\A{} The browser breaks it again at other widths.
\end{document}
