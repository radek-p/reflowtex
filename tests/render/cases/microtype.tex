% Character protrusion and font expansion, which the viewer reproduces.
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{microtype}
\begin{document}
Protrusion lets punctuation and hyphens hang into the margin, so that the
margin looks straight to the eye, and expansion lets the line breaker widen
or narrow the letters a little, so that fewer words are hyphenated and the
spaces between words are more even. ``Quotation marks,'' commas, full
stops -- and dashes -- are the characters that protrude the most.
\end{document}
