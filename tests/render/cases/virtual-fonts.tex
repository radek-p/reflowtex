% Virtual fonts: the ae package sets T1 text in Computer Modern through
% virtual fonts, each accented letter an accent and a letter its packet puts
% in place (an acute moved left over an e). Each glyph is drawn from the font
% the packet names, where the packet puts it.
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage[T1]{fontenc}
\usepackage{ae}
\begin{document}
Café, naïve, façade, señor, Gödel, Dvořák, Ångström and Łukasiewicz: the
accented letters of a T1 font are built here from an accent and a letter,
moved into place by the virtual font, while plain letters are drawn as they
are. «Œuvres complètes», thé à la crème, smørrebrød, Øresund, Straße.
\end{document}
