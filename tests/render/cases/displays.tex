The Gaussian integral is
\[ \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}, \]
and the Basel problem asks for the sum
\begin{equation}\label{eq:basel}
  \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6}.
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:basel} was proved by Euler. A short line then
\[ a = b \]
and a display that ends the paragraph:
\begin{equation}
  \zeta(s) = \prod_{p} \frac{1}{1 - p^{-s}}.
\end{equation}
