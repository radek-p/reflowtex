% \rightskip to every line, so their stretch counts in each line's badness
% and shares its slack with the spaces. An author block centred with finite
% stretch (0pt plus .3\hsize on both sides, a rigid \parfillskip, as some
% classes set their front matter): its lines are shorter than \centering's
% and its spaces stretch too. Then LaTeX's \centering, \raggedright and
% \raggedleft, a ragged right of finite stretch (0pt plus 2em), a \leftskip
% of 2em plus 1fil, and a table of contents.
