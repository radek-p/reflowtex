% Paragraphs whose lines differ in measure: \hangindent with positive and negative \hangafter, hung on the left and on the right, an explicit \parshape, a shape resumed after a display, and numbered section titles long enough to wrap, whose later lines LaTeX hangs by the number's width.
\documentclass{article}
% one page with nothing but the text: checked against plain lualatex too (cases.ts)
\pagestyle{empty}
\begin{document}
\section{A section title long enough to wrap onto a second line, which hangs by the number}

\hangindent=3em \hangafter=2
A paragraph whose first two lines are set across the whole column and whose
later lines are indented by three ems from the left, as \verb|\hangafter=2|
asks, long enough to run on to a fourth or fifth line of the column, so that
several of its lines are hung and a few are not, gladly and quickly.

\hangindent=-6em \hangafter=-3
A paragraph whose first three lines stop six ems short of the right edge, as
a picture beside them would make them, with \verb|\hangindent=-6em| and
\verb|\hangafter=-3|, and whose later lines take the whole column again, long
enough to run on to a fifth line, with words like overfull and justified.

\parshape 4 0pt 345pt 2em 300pt 4em 260pt 1em 330pt
A paragraph with an explicit \verb|\parshape| of four lines: the first across
the column, the second indented two ems and narrower, the third four ems and
narrower still, and every line after the fourth as the fourth is, one em in
from the left; long enough to have six or seven lines, so that its last
pair holds for several of them, quickly and gladly, as TeX sets it.

\hangindent=4em \hangafter=-8
A paragraph hung on the left for eight lines, interrupted by a display after
a few words of its text:
\[ a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \]
and resumed after it, where its line numbers go on from the lines before the
display and the display's three, so that only a few more lines are hung
before the rest take the whole column again, as TeX numbers them, with
enough text after the display to run on to several lines.

\section{Another section title, long enough to wrap onto a second line of the column at this width}
A last paragraph after the second title, long enough to break onto a second
line of the column.
\end{document}
