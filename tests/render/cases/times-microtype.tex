% Times through its virtual fonts under microtype, as in real papers: T1 Times
% (times), whose ligatures ff, ffi and ffl a virtual font builds from two
% characters, and whose small capitals are characters moved right by a little
% letterspacing. TeX protrudes and expands each as the one character it set:
% headings in small capitals hang their first character into the margin, and
% justified lines widen their ligatures with their other letters. The headings
% are short enough to stay on one line at every width tested.
