% multicols, as in real papers: its column lines go down the main list one by one, and its output routine then puts the balanced columns back as one box. The page shows the column lines; the strip ships them once, not again in the box. A \columnbreak and two environments, between paragraphs at the text width.
\documentclass[11pt]{article}
\usepackage{multicol}
% multicol breaks its paragraphs at a tolerance and an \emergencystretch of its
% own; here they are the document's, so that the case is about the columns
\multicoltolerance=\tolerance \multicolpretolerance=\pretolerance
\renewcommand*\setemergencystretch[2]{}
\begin{document}
A first paragraph at the text width, long enough to fill a line and then some
more words, with a descender to end on: quickly.

\begin{multicols}{2}
Text in two columns, long enough to make several lines in a column of half the
width, so that the columns are balanced at the end of the environment.

A second paragraph in the columns, with more words to set and a descender to
end on: quickly.
\end{multicols}

A paragraph between the two environments, long enough to fill a line of the
text width and then some more.

\begin{multicols}{3}
Three columns this time, with a short paragraph.
\columnbreak

And one after a forced column break, with a few more words to set.
\end{multicols}

A last paragraph after the columns, long enough to fill a line of the text
width and then some more words.
\end{document}
