% Fonts with only a METAFONT source (bbm's double-struck letters): drawn from METAFONT's bitmaps, in text and in displays.
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{amsmath,bbm}
\begin{document}
Double-struck letters set by \verb|\mathbbm| come from a font that has no
outline of its own, only its METAFONT source: the real numbers
$\mathbbm{R}$, the integers $\mathbbm{Z}$, the naturals $\mathbbm{N}$ and the
indicator function $\mathbbm{1}_A$ of a set $A \subseteq \mathbbm{R}^n$, with
the sans-serif form $\mathbbmss{N}$ and $\mathbbmss{1}$ beside them, in a
paragraph long enough to break across lines differently at every width.
\[
  \mathbbm{1}_{[0,1]}(x) = \begin{cases} 1 & x \in [0,1], \\ 0 & x \in \mathbbm{R} \setminus [0,1], \end{cases}
  \qquad \mathbbm{E}\,X = \sum_{k \in \mathbbm{N}} k\, \mathbbm{P}(X = k).
\]
And after the display, a last line with $\mathbbm{C}$ and $\mathbbm{Q}$ in it.
\end{document}
