% Rows with no space between them: a longtable, whose rows TeX stacks with no glue at all (\baselineskip and \lineskip are 0 inside it), a short \halign of struts under \offinterlineskip, and a paragraph set with \baselineskip=0pt.
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{array,longtable}
\begin{document}
A paragraph before the table, long enough to fill a line of the text width
and then a few more words, so that it wraps.
\begin{longtable}{|l|l|}\hline
Name & Value \\ \hline\hline
alpha & one \\
beta & two \\
gamma & three \\ \hline
delta & four \\ \hline
\end{longtable}
A paragraph between the table and a short alignment of struts, long enough
to break onto a second line of the column.

{\offinterlineskip
\halign{\strut#\hfil\quad&#\hfil\cr
first&row\cr
second&row, with a descender: y\cr
third&row\cr}}
A paragraph set with no baselineskip, so that TeX puts only the lineskip
between its lines. {\baselineskip=0pt It goes on long enough to break onto
several lines of the column, at every width it is shown at, gladly.\par}
And a last paragraph after it, long enough to fill a line of the text width
and then a few more words.
\end{document}
