% Paragraphs with glue of infinite stretch inside them: \hfill between two
% boxes too wide for one line, boxes joined by \hskip 1em plus .17fil (LaTeX's
% \and between authors), \hfill and \hfil between words (a break there, and a
% justified line after it), a break at a penalty before an \hfill, and \\ in
% the middle of a paragraph, whose \hfil follows a \nobreak. Each paragraph
% is set at several measures. Captured with capture.lua into fills/
% (README.md).
