% Paragraphs set between margin skips that stretch or shrink: TeX adds
% \leftskip and \rightskip to every line, so their stretch and shrink count in
% each line's badness and share its slack. Centred with finite stretch
% (0pt plus .3\hsize on both sides and a rigid \parfillskip, as some classes
% centre their author blocks), LaTeX's \centering, \raggedright and
% \raggedleft (0pt plus 1fil), a ragged right of finite stretch (0pt plus
% 2em), a \leftskip of 2em plus 1fil, margin skips that shrink, and a table
% of contents' entry (\rightskip 2.55em, \parfillskip -\rightskip, so that
% its last line reaches into the margin skip; the dots are left out, a
% \hfill in their place). Each paragraph is set at
% several measures. Captured with capture.lua into margins/ (README.md).
