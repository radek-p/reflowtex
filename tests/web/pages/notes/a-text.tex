The harmonic series $\sum_{n\ge1} 1/n$ diverges, though very slowly: its
partial sums grow like the logarithm of the number of terms.\footnote{More
precisely, like $\ln N + \gamma$, where $\gamma \approx 0.5772$.} Its terms
tend to zero, which is not enough, as
\begin{equation}\label{eq:basel}
  \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6}
\end{equation}
shows by contrast: equation~\eqref{eq:basel} converges.\footnote{Euler, 1734.}
