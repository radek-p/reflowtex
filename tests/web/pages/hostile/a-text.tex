A page's own stylesheet may be written for other content: here it scales
every picture to its container, gives every block a margin and doubles the
line height. The Moon's distance changes over a month \mypopover{more}{From
about 363\,300\,km at perigee to 405\,500\,km at apogee.}, and the text
goes on, with \rotatebox{30}{rotated} text in a line and a link to
\href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basel_problem}{the Basel problem and its
long history} that breaks across lines at a narrow width.
