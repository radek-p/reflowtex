\section{Two views}\label{sec:views}
A block may be shown in two places at once, each laid out at its own
width: the basket holds \webtext{fruit}{no fruit at all} in both of them,
and the words around it are broken into lines again whenever the page
changes it. This paragraph goes on long enough to be broken into several
lines at any width the tests use, so that the two views break it in
different places, and a mark made in one of them can reach across a line
break without showing in the other. Section~\ref{sec:views} is where this
paragraph stands, whichever view it is read in.
