\section{Alpha}\label{sec:alpha}
The Basel problem asks for
\begin{equation}\label{eq:one}
  \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6},
\end{equation}
and equation~\eqref{eq:one} of Section~\ref{sec:alpha} answers it.
{{< /latex >}}
