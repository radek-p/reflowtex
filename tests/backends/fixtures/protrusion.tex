\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{fontspec}
\usepackage[protrusion=true,expansion=false]{microtype}
\begin{document}
A paragraph long enough to break into lines, with punctuation at their ends:
commas, full stops, hyphens - and quotation marks, so that protrusion has
characters to hang into the margin, line after line, as microtype sets it.
\end{document}
